Sample Attainment

The target to be achieved for each course includes two parameters:

  1. Expected Proﬁciency /Knowledge (EP) :-

It is the grade secured by more than 50% of the total number of students in the previous university examinations.

  1. Expected Attainment (EA) :-

Eg: The Previous Attainment based on previous University Examinations is 74.46% students can score the Expected Proficiency of D grade. Expected Attainment is fixed as 12% improvement over a six year period considering 2% improvement every year.
