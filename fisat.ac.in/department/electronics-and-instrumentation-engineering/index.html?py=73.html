FISAT wins Xplore 2023-education competition organized by German Multinational -Phoenix Contact

The team from the Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering Department of Federal Institute of Science & Technology (FISAT), Angamaly, Kochi, won the Xplore Technology Award 2023 in category “Smart Technology” for a sustainable world for their project “Water management at home for a sustainable world using IoT.” The project was developed by a team of students led by Aaron Sabu Joseph, Aakash Shaji, Amala Joy, Allen Simon Antony, Clinto Jose, Aravind Surendran . Lakshmi Nandakumar and Dr. Abi P Mathew were their mentors.

The team won the Xplore Technology Award 2023 by competing against 700 other teams from around the world. The award ceremony was held at the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action in Berlin on October 20, 2023, which coincided with Phoenix Contact’s 100th anniversary. The winners were honored by Minister Michael Kellner, Parliamentary State Secretary.

The team’s project was judged as a significant contribution to the effort to conserve water as it has the potential to save a significant amount of water, which is important in light of the global water crisis. The project was considered as innovative, as it uses IoT to monitor and control water usage and as a new and innovative way to conserve water.

The team’s project was referred to as a great example of how technology can be used to solve real-world problems and also a great example of how students can make a difference in the world.

The vegetables are directly from the farmers and is run in collaboration with manjapra forane church agriculture market. The market will be live on Mondays and Thursdays. SPICES FUND

Department of Electronics &Instrumentation received funding from AICTE-Scheme for Promoting Interests, Creativity, and Ethics among Students (SPICES) for department’s Students Association, Instrumentation`s Dextrous Etudiants Association(IDEA) for the year 2021-22.

The objective of SPICES is to energize and position students club as facilitating entity for pursuit of individual interests, creative work, showcasing talent, networking and teamwork opportunities, social experience; organization and management skills, exposure to professional ethics etc.

Department has planned yearlong activities for effective utilisation of fund received for the benefit of students of FISAT as a whole. The proposal for funding were initiated by Dr. Abi P Mathew, Ms. Sreevidya P and Mr. Anil Johny.
