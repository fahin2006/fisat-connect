Faculty Profile

Dr. Basil K Jeemon
Assistant Professor (Special Grade)
Electronics and Communication Engineering

Profile

Basil K. Jeemon completed his B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Viswajyothi College of Engineering and Technology, Vazhakulam, in 2011. He completed his M.Tech in Communication Engineering from VIT University in 2013. He completed his Ph.D. from the School of Engineering, Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), in the domain of physical layer algorithms in wireless communication networks. His areas of interest include signal processing for communications, Physical layer algorithms in wireless communication systems, and high-frequency antenna design. He has taught courses related to electromagnetics, communication systems, and signal processing. He is a Life Member of ISTE and a Professional Member of IEEE.
