Faculty Profile

Dr. Jyothish K John
DEAN and Controller of Examinations
Computer Science and Engineering

Profile

Dr. Jyothish K. John is presently working as Controller of Examinations and Professor in Computer Science and Engineering. He earned a Bachelor of Engineering (BE) in 2003 from M.S. University, a Master’s in Computer Science and Engineering in 2007 from Karunya University in Coimbatore, and Ph.D. in 2018 from the Noorul Islam Centre for Higher Education. He joined FISAT in June 2004. His research interests pertain to the areas of Computer Networks, Distributed Systems and Cloud Computing.  Dr. Jyothish K. John has published more than 18 research papers in various international journals and conferences. He has also been granted a patent. He has 19 years of teaching experience. He is a resource person and has handled expert classes and workshops in the areas of Cloud Computing, Network Simulators and Distributed Computing. He has attended more than 15 workshops and conferences. Dr. Jyothish K. John is a Certified AWS Cloud Practitioner. He has served as a reviewer for international journals and as a session chair for prestigious international conferences. He has organized 3 International conferences. The institution has recognized his contribution by Presenting the ‘Best Teacher Award’ in 2016.
