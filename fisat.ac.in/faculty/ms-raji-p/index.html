Faculty Profile

Ms. Raji P
Assistant Professor (Special Grade)
Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering

Profile

 

Ms Raji P is currently serving as an Assistant Professor (special grade) in the Department of Electronics and Instrumentation FISAT. She is pursuing her PhD at the Karunya Institute of Engineering. She completed her M.Tech in Applied Electronics and Instrumentation in the year 2010 and B.Tech from Mahatma Gandhi University in 2007. 

She started her career in  FISAT in the year 2013 as an Assistant professor. Before that, she served as a lecturer in Adi Shankara Institute of Engineering in the year 2008-2009. 

Mrs Raji P is the co-founder of the start-up Rastelx Technologies Private Limited and holds the position of Director of the company since September 2022. 

At FISAT she is holding the position of the start-up coordinator of the IIC at the institute level. She is actively involved in the entrepreneurship development activities of the institute and coordinates the training and workshops related to it. She is providing internships for students of the FISAT in the area of mobile application development.

Awards and honours

 

      • Smart India Hackathon

    Smart India Hackathon is a nationwide initiative to provide students a platform to solve some of the pressing problems we face in our daily lives and thus inculcate a culture of product innovation and a mindset of problem-solving. Mrs. Raji P has mentored the students who entered the grand finale of SIH software edition 2020 under the theme of student innovation. The team also got to interact with the honorable Prime Minister of India during the finale. He congratulated the team and the mentor for guiding such socially relevant projects. The team also entered into the final 5. The team consisted of  Athulya Sreekumar, M Vishnu, Yadu Krishnan K S, Raichu Mary Mathew from the Department of Electronics and Instrumentation (S8 EIE) and Rishad ( S8 CSE ).

      • Lilavati award

    AICTE announced the Lilavati award with women empowerment as the theme, the objective of the award is to create awareness about issues like sanitation, hygiene, health, nutrition, literacy, employment, technology, credit, marketing, innovation, skill development, natural resources, and rights among women. Mrs. Raji P and Dr Jiji Antony (Civil Engineering) applied for the awards in the sub-theme “Women sanitation and hygiene” and FISAT was the only engineering college from Kerala that entered into the finals under the theme. FISAT entered into the final 10 teams nominated for the award. The team consisted of Athulya Sreekumar, M Vishnu, and Yadu Krishnan K S (S8 EIE)

      • Harvard Grant project

    Mrs Raji P won a grant of Rs 25000 in   Health Care from Harvard Medical School and Boston Children’s Hospital for her research work. Mrs Raji P is pursuing her PhD at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences in the field of Biomedical Engineering

      • Toycathon 2021 evaluator

    Toycathon 2021 is an inter-ministerial initiative organized by the Ministry of Education’s Innovation Cell with support from the All India Council for Technical Education, the Ministry of Women and Child Development, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, the Ministry of MSME, the Ministry of Textiles and Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. Mrs Raji P was the evaluator of the prestigious program and she has evaluated more than ten teams from the school category.

      • UV sanitisation unit for Taluk Hospital

    The UV sanitization unit designed and manufactured by Mrs. Raji P and Mr. Ajay Baby (S8 EIE)  was delivered to the Taluk Hospital Angamaly on 7 July 2020. The device can be used for sanitization of N95 masks as well as the watches, phones, etc used by doctors, nurses, and the healthcare workers there.

      • Best paper award

    Mrs. Raji P has been awarded the Best Paper for her paper “ Machine Learning based diagnosis of diseases associated with abnormal and heavy menstrual bleeding: A Literature Review” in the International Conference on Novel Approaches and Developments in Biomedical Engineering – ICDNABE 2021 Organized by the Department of Biomedical Engineering of Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, Coimbatore during 22nd and 23rd April 2021.

      • SHE POWER HACKATHON

    Mrs Raji P ,Mrs Arathi E and Ms Lakshmi Nandakumar (Assistant professor, Department EIE) received the special mention award for PINKPAL MOBILE APP FOR WOMEN SECURITY in the year 2020. The hackathon was conducted by KSUM and the US state department for the empowerment of women.

      • Women productization grant of 6 lakhs INR

    Mrs Raji P and Mrs Honey Devassy (Assistant professor, department of EIE) received 6 lakhs grant for their start-up incubated in Federal Labz FISAT. The grant is for the development of their society security mobile app named COMPANION SOS

      • RKVY RAFTAAR grant of 5 lakhs INR

    The startup founded by the team from FISAT was granted 5 lakhs for the development of their mobile app named SEVAFARM from the ministry of Agriculture under the RKVY RAFTAAR program

      • NEC MENTOR OF IITB

    Mrs. Raji P is selected as the mentor of the National Entrepreneurship program conducted by IITB in the year 2023. She is serving as the mentor of two colleges St Aloysius college Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh and Symbiosis institute of technology, Pune, Maharashtra. She is guiding the ECELL in the colleges in strategizing and planning their events.

Administrative & Institutional Support

 

  • NSS and Women Cell activities

Mrs. Raji P has associated with NSS for the distribution of posters to health centers all over Kerala for the popularization of the “One Rupee One Pad” scheme under the Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Jan Aushadhi Pariyojna (PMBJP). She has also coordinated the conduct of a webinar by the Women Cell FISAT “Let’s talk about menstruation and menstrual health”, which was attended by more than 300 women. The webinar was conducted to raise awareness among women about the physical, mental and social well being associated with menstruation.

  • Entrepreneurship development programs

She attended Entrepreneurship development programs for two months which was conducted by the Ministry of Agriculture RKVY RAAFTAR in the year 2022. She also received certification for entrepreneurship from Google start-up school in the year 2023. She was a semi-finalist of the TiE women Global Pitchfest Competition 2023. She participated in the accelerate her program of AWS and won an AWS credit of 2000$ in the year 2023. She participated in the HUDDLE GLOBAL startup expo in the year 2023 and TiECON Kerala event 2023. She is a cohort member of the Wadhwani Liftoff program for 2023 2024.
