Faculty Profile

Dr.Rinku Scaria
Assistant Professor(Special Grade)
Electrical and Electronics Engineering

Profile

Dr.Rinku Scaria received the B.Tech. degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from the Mahatma Gandhi University Kottayam, Kerala, India, in 2010, and the M.E Degree in Power Systems Engineering from the Annamalai University,Tamilnadu ,India, in 2012 .She received her Ph.D from Annamalai University,Tamilnadu ,India. She Started her career as Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering , Federal Institute of Science and Technology,Kerala, in June 2012.she has taught subjects including Basic Electrical Engineering, FACTs, Advanced power system stability, Distributed generation and smart grid and Microgrid. She has completed Ph.D from Annamalai University in 2024 February .Her research interests include Grid Connected Power Systems, Microgrids, Renewable energy, and Flexible ac transmission systems.She is a reviewer of Electric Power Components and Systems(Taylor and Francis).Patent  Hydrapure – Smart Portable Filter Bottle published.
