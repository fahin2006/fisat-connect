Faculty Profile

Dr.Sreevidya P
Associate Professor
Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering

Profile

Dr. Sreevidya P has done her B.Tech from NSS College of Engineering, Palakkad and M.E in Control Systems from PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore. She completed her PhD in multimodal fusion techniques from Amritha Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore. She has 3.8 years of industrial experience and 20 years of teaching experience.

Her areas of interests are image processing, control algorithms, process control and AI/ML. She is a passionate teacher and enthusiast in learning new technologies. Her interests are student mentoring and academic research activities.

She has published around 25 papers and one patent in her credit and bagged awards in several project competitions.
