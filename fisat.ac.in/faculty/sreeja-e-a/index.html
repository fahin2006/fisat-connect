Ms.Sreeja E A
Assistant Professor(Senior Grade)
Electrical and Electronics Engineering

Profile

Ms. Sreeja E.A., completed her B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Govt. Engineering College ,Thrissur and M.Tech in Electrical Engineering with specialization in Power electronics from School of Engineering, CUSAT. She started her career as guest Lecturer in Electrical Engineering at Govt. Engineering College ,Thrissur, Kerala in 1998. In 2004, she joined SNGCE, Kadayiruppu as Lecturer. Since 2006, she has been working as Assistant Professor in Electrical Engineering at Federal Institute of Science and Technology, Angamaly, Kerala, India. Over a period of time, she has taught many subjects including Control Systems, Electrical Machines, Electrical System Design, Power Electronics, Linear System Analysis, Electrical Drives and Control. Currently she is pursuing her PhD at Cochin University.
