Ms. Neha Beegam P.E(On Contract)
Assistant Professor
Computer Science and Engineering

Profile

Ms. Neha Beegam P E is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT), Kerala, with expertise in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Blockchain Technology, and Cybersecurity. She completed her B.Tech and M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering under Mahatma Gandhi University and is currently pursuing her PhD in Computer Science and Engineering at Bharath Institute of Higher Education and Research. With extensive teaching and research experience across reputed institutions, she has actively contributed to academic excellence through publications in Scopus-indexed journals, conference presentations, patent contributions, FDP participations, and technical event coordination. Her research interests include intelligent healthcare systems, mental health analysis using AI, computer vision, blockchain-based authentication systems, and data analytics. She is also actively involved in mentoring student projects, organizing workshops, and serving as a reviewer and resource person for various academic and technical events.
