Ms. Gayathri I K
Assistant Professor (Special Grade)
Computer Science and Engineering

Profile

Mrs. Gayathri I K completed her B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from College of Engineering Attingal in the year 2008. She completed M. Tech in Communication Engineering from FISAT in the year 2014. Currently doing PhD at NITC. She joined as Assistant Professor in FISAT on 6th August 2014. Her areas of interest are ‘wireless communication’ and ‘image processing’. She has taken subjects relating to spectral analysis, communication and logic circuits.
