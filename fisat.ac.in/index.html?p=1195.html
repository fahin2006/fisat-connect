Dr. Arun Kumar M N
Professor
Computer Science and Engineering

Profile

 

Dr.Arun Kumar M N is presently working as Professor in the department of Computer Science and Engineering. He completed Bachelor of Engineering (BE) from University of Mysore in 1996, M.Sc ( Engg.) from VTU in 2006, Belgaum, Karnataka and Ph.D from University of Mysore in 2013. His research interest pertains to the areas of Image Processing, Data Mining and Machine Learning. Dr. Arun kumar M N has published more than 38 research papers in various international journals and conferences.  He has 27 years of teaching experience. He is a life member of CSI and ISTE and has attended more than 25 Seminars/Workshops/Conferences.

Dr.Arun Kumar M N is recognized as a Ph.D Research Supervisor by Kerala Technological University (KTU), Kerala and Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Karnataka and is guiding 8 Ph.D research scholars. He is a reviewer of some international journals and was session chair for some reputed international conferences. He has organized 4 international conferences and 2 Faculty Development Programmes. He was awarded the Best Teacher award from FISAT in the year 2021. He is a member of Board of Studies, CSE (Allied), KTU.</P
