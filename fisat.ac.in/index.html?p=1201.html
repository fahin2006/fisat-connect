Faculty Profile

Dr. Paul P Mathai
Professor & HoD
Computer Science and Engineering

Profile

Dr. Paul P Mathai is presently working as a Professor and Head of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, FISAT. He received his Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Engineering from University of Madras, India in 2004. Then he obtained his Master’s degree in Computer and Information Technology from Manonmanium Sundaranar University, India. In 2018, he received his Doctor of Philosophy in Computer Science and Engineering from Noorul Islam Centre for Higher Education, Kanyakumari, India. He started his career as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Adi Shankara Institute of Engineering & Technology, Kalady, India during the period June 2005 to January 2011. He was working as Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Federal Institute of Science and Technology, Angamaly from January 2011 to February 2019. From March 1st, 2019 to October 30th, 2019 he was working as Assistant Professor (Senior Grade). He was promoted to Associate Professor on November 1st, 2019 and on June 1st, 2025  he was promoted to Professor. He has been serving as the Head of the Department since January 1st, 2025. He is an approved supervisor of Kerala Technological University (KTU) and currently he is guiding 5 research scholars (Part time). He has published 45 research papers in various International Journals/Conferences. He has published 2 books and 4 patents also. He is a Life member of ISTE and IAENG. He is acting as a reviewer in various scopus indexed International Journals. He has been selected as an External Academic Auditor of Kerala Technological University (KTU) from 2019 onwards. His specializations include Data Mining, Database Management Systems, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
