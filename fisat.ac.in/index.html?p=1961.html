Faculty Profile

Mr. Anoop E G
Assistant Professor (Senior Grade)
Electronics and Communication Engineering

Profile

Mr. Anoop E. G. completed his B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Cochin University College of Engineering, Kuttanad (CUSAT) in 2004 and obtained his M.E. in VLSI Design from Karunya University, Coimbatore, in 2008. He began his academic career as a Lecturer at Cochin University College of Engineering, Kuttanad, in January 2005. Subsequently, he served as a Lecturer at Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT) from November 2005 to August 2006. He joined the Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT) as a Lecturer on 16 June 2008.

He has organized and conducted several national-level talks, seminars, and workshops. He served as the Staff Coordinator of ECHO, the students’ association of the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, from 2015 to 2020, and as the Treasurer of ECHO from 2008 to 2020. He worked as the Staff-in-Charge of Sanguine Workers and Admirers of Nature (SWAN), the Nature Club of FISAT, from 2020 to 2024, and also served as the Staff Coordinator of the Anti-Ragging Committee from 2024 to 2025. Currently, he is serving as the Staff-in-Charge of the Students’ Union.

Mr. Anoop E. G. is an active team member of Project Ekalavya – IIT Bombay, Spoken Tutorial Courses, ASAP courses, and various IEEE activities. He is a Life Member of ISTE.
