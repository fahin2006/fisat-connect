Ms. Sheelu Susan Mathews
Assistant Professor(Special Grade)
Electronics and Communication Engineering

Profile

Ms. Sheelu Susan Mathews has completed her B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Mar Baselios College of Engineering And Technology, Trivandrum in the year 2009. She completed M.tech in VLSI and  Embedded Systems from ER and DCI IT, Trivandrum in the year 2011. She joined as Asst. Professor in FISAT on 02nd November 2011. She is currently pursuing part time Phd in Biomedical Signal Processing using AI from BMS College of Engineering, affiliated to VTU.
