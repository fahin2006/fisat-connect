Faculty Profile

Ms. Dhanya S
Assistant Professor (Special Grade)
Electronics and Communication Engineering

Profile

Ms. Dhanya S. has completed her B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering from Government Engineering College, Wayanad in the year 2008. She completed M. Tech in Communication Engineering from Federal Institute of Science & Technology, Angamaly in the year 2012. She has worked as Graduate Apprentice at Kerala State IT Mission, Thiruvananthapuram for a period of one year and has associated with the SPARK project of the Kerala Government. She joined as Assistant Professor in FISAT on 05/07/2013. She is currently pursuing PhD from APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University. She is a lifetime member of ISTE. She has authored one paper in SpringerPlus, one SCIE-indexed Elsevier journal publication, and several papers in reputed international journals, in addition to seven publications in IEEE Xplore and other indexed international conferences.
