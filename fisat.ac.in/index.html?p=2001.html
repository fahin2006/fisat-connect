Faculty Profile

Ms. Sreelekshmy S
Assistant Professor (Special Grade)
Electronics and Communication Engineering

Profile

Ms. Sreelekshmy S. is a 2012 M.Tech alumna of Amrita School of Engineering, Kollam, where she specialized in VLSI Design. She is currently pursuing her Ph.D. in VLSI Design at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Ettimadai. She obtained her B.Tech degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Sree Buddha College of Engineering, Pattoor, Alappuzha, in 2008.

She began her professional career as a Programmer Analyst at Infovista Technology, Kochi, in 2008, and subsequently served as a Technical Support Engineer at C-Cubed Solutions, Bengaluru. Before joining the Federal Institute of Science and Technology as an Assistant Professor in September 2014, she worked as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at UKF College of Engineering and Technology.

During her tenure at UKF College of Engineering, she guided a project titled “Search and Rescue Robot with ARM (SARA)”, which received a scholarship grant of INR 2 lakhs from the Kerala State Council for Science, Technology and Environment. Over the years, she has taught a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses, including Analog Integrated Circuit Design I, Analog Circuits II, Digital Signal Processing, Signals and Communication Systems, VLSI Design, Fuzzy Systems, and TV and Radar Engineering.

Her research interests include Analog Electronics and Low-Power VLSI Design. She is a lifetime member of the Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE) and has published four papers in reputed international journals.
