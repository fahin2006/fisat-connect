Dr. Rejeesh C R
Professor & HoD
Mechanical Engineering

Profile

Dr. Rejeesh C R (PhD, FIE) is currently a Professor at Federal Institute of Science and Technology, Kochi. He was awarded PhD by the faculty of engineering at Cochin University of Science and Technology in 2018.

He is also an approved research supervisor at APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University, Kerala and is a former member of the curriculum committee for the B.Tech programme at the University.  He has close interactions with various Professional Institutions over research and development of Advanced Materials, their Processing and Characterization.

He is a Fellow of The Institution of Engineers (India) and is a life member of the prestigious Indian Science Congress Association, a professional body under the Department of Science & Technology, Government of India. He is also a member of various professional bodies at the National and International level, such as the Society of Wood Science and Technology, Wisconsin, USA and the Indian Society for Technical Education.
