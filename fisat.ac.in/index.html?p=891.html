Ms.Rakhee.R
Assistant Professor(Senior Grade)
Electrical and Electronics Engineering

Profile

Ms. Rakhee R completed her B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Muslim Association College of Engineering, Trivandrum and M.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering with specialisation in Power Electronics from School of Engineering, CUSAT. She started her career as Lecturer on contract basis in SOE, CUSAT. Since 2008, She is working as an Assistant Professor in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Federal Institute of Science and Technology, Angamaly, Kerala, India. She is currently pursuing her Ph.D at SOE, CUSAT. Her areas of interest include Power Electronic Converters, Drives, Power Quality Improvement in Grid connected Systems, Role of power electronic systems in power systems.
